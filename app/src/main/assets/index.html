1. Warranty Duration & Component Dependency

Fixfly Innovations Pvt. Ltd. provides a limited hardware warranty of up to 6 Months on replacement spare parts installed by our certified engineers. However, the exact warranty period is not uniform for all items and depends directly on the specific hardware product, component category, brand, and official manufacturer terms.

  • Solid-State Drives (SSD) & RAM: Covered by 3 Years to 5 Years brand warranty (e.g., Crucial, Kingston, Western Digital, Samsung).
  • Laptop Screens & Display Panels: 3 Months to 6 Months replacement warranty against dead display lines, white screens, and internal circuit failure (physical crack excluded).
  • Batteries & Power Adapters: 3 Months to 6 Months warranty against charging dropouts or battery swelling.
  • Keyboards & Trackpads: 3 Months warranty against unresponsive keys or matrix failure.
  • Chip-Level Motherboard Repairs: 1 Month to 3 Months warranty on reworked power rails and micro-soldered SMD components.

2. Free Replacement of Defective Parts

Under active warranty coverage, if a replacement part installed by Fixfly fails or ceases functioning due to an inherent manufacturing fault, bad memory sector, or internal component breakdown, the replacement part is provided 100% Free of charge. The customer is not billed for the replacement component.

3. Home Visit, Inspection & Installation Charges Policy

Important Terms Regarding Doorstep Engineer Logistics:

While the defective replacement part itself is provided 100% free of charge under valid warranty, doorstep engineer home visits, inspection diagnostics, travel logistics, and technician re-installation labor are NOT covered free of charge.

Doorstep visit and installation labor charges depend on the customer's locality, distance from our nearest city dispatch hub, and the technical complexity of the disassembly/installation procedure. Customers may also opt to hand over the device at our verified city hub to minimize visit logistical fees.

4. Conditions That Void Warranty (Exclusions)

The limited warranty becomes null and void under the following circumstances:

  • Physical damage, drops, hairline screen fractures, damaged connector pins, or structural chassis cracks.
  • Liquid ingress, water spills, moisture corrosion, rust on PCB traces, or chemical cleaning accidents.
  • Electrical power surges, non-standard adapter usage, or lightning-induced voltage burnouts.
  • Tampering, disassembly, or repair attempts by any third-party technician, unauthorized service center, or customer.
  • Tampered, altered, or missing Fixfly warranty security stickers or manufacturer serial barcodes.
  • Software-induced issues, operating system corruption, ransomware, virus infections, or unauthorized BIOS flashes.
